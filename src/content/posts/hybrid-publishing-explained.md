## Three models, plainly

| Model | Who pays | Who controls rights |
|---|---|---|
| Traditional | Publisher | Publisher |
| Hybrid | Author | Usually author |
| Self-published | Author | Author |

Ask any hybrid publisher these questions before signing:

- What are the distribution terms, specifically?
- Who owns the files at the end?
- What happens if the book sells well?

## The first page problem

Most submissions are rejected before the end of the first page. Writers hear
this and conclude they need something explosive up front.

That is the wrong lesson.

## What actually holds attention

An editor is reading for **control** — evidence that the writer knows which
details matter and which do not.

> A confident first paragraph is not a loud one. It is one where nothing is
> wasted.

### Three things that signal control

1. Specificity that earns its place
2. Sentences of varying length, deliberately
3. A narrator who knows more than they are saying
